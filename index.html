Visual Identity Direction

Colours
Saffron · Sage · Ivory · Deep Ink
Saffron (consciousness, warmth, the Mother's love). Sage green (nature, growth, the body's vitality). Ivory (purity, space, the calm of inner focus). Deep ink (authority, depth, the written wisdom of Sri Aurobindo).
Typography
Serif Wisdom + Clean Body
Use a serif typeface for quotes, titles, and moments of depth (Cormorant, EB Garamond). Pair with a clean sans-serif for captions and body text. Never bold generic fonts.
Photography
Warm, Natural, Unfiltered
Camp activities in natural light. Children's faces mid-discovery. Teachers in quiet reflection. Nature details — trees, hands, feet on earth. Avoid stock images and posed perfection.
Design Feel
Contemplative, Not Clinical
Generous whitespace. Organic compositions. Textures that feel like handmade paper or worn cloth. Quote overlays on photography. Mandalic or natural geometry as motifs.

Tone — Do

  • Speak with reverence and warmth
  • Use The Mother's quotes as anchors
  • Share transformation, not performance
  • Invite, never preach
  • Celebrate inner growth quietly

Tone — Don't

  • Use corporate education jargon
  • Post generic "motivational" graphics
  • Overclaim or compare to other orgs
  • Reduce Integral Education to a curriculum
  • Use aggressive call-to-action language
02 · Brand Story
A Century of Wisdom.
A Living Mission.
SAFIER does not begin with a board meeting or a marketing plan. It begins in Puducherry, in the first half of the 20th century, when two extraordinary seers — Sri Aurobindo and The Mother — envisioned an education that was not merely about knowledge, but about the complete unfoldment of the human being.

The Vision is Born in Puducherry

Sri Aurobindo and The Mother conceived Integral Education as a path of conscious evolution — nurturing the physical, vital, mental, psychic, and spiritual dimensions of every child. In 1943, The Mother founded the Sri Aurobindo International Centre of Education in Pondicherry as a living laboratory of this vision.

Sri Aurobindo Society Carries the Flame

The Sri Aurobindo Society, formed under The Mother's direct guidance, became the organisational body tasked with spreading this light beyond Puducherry. For decades, it has carried the authentic lineage of Integral Education — not as theory, but as lived practice.

SAFIER: Bringing IE Into the World

SAFIER — the Sri Aurobindo Foundation for Integral Education and Research — was born from the need to make this wisdom accessible to educators, schools, parents, and children across India and beyond. It became the bridge between ancient insight and modern classrooms.

Annual Workshops, Children's Camps, School Partnerships

Through annual Integral Education Workshops for teachers and principals, transformative Children's Camps, and school implementation programs, SAFIER creates direct encounters with a different kind of education — one where the inner life of the child is honoured as much as academics.

The Call Continues — Join the Great Adventure

As The Mother said: "I invite you to the great adventure." SAFIER now invites a new generation of educators, parents, and changemakers to discover that the most powerful thing we can do for a child is nurture their inner being. The journey continues — and there is room for you.

For the next 2 months, the core social media audience should be educators (teachers & principals) and conscious parents — people who are actively engaged in a child's world and are open to a different approach. Secondary focus: young adults who may volunteer, attend camps, or carry Integral Education into their professional lives.
